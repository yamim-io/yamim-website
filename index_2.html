The problem

Two numbers explain why margins never move

Aquaculture is the fastest-growing food-production sector on Earth — and almost none of that growth reaches the bottom line. These are the two structural reasons why.

60%
of operating costs go straight to feed

Feed is the single largest line item a farm runs — bigger than labor, water, or equipment combined. Move that number, and the rest of the P&L moves with it.

Total
dependency on third-party feed

An input a farm can't produce itself is an input it can't control. Feed quality drives FCR (feed conversion ratio), fish health, and water quality all at once — outsourcing feed means outsourcing margin, yield, and water together.

Supplier bottlenecks cap how fast a farm can scale Unsustainable feed raises nitrite and degrades farm water Margins stay flat even as the market moves toward $650B
